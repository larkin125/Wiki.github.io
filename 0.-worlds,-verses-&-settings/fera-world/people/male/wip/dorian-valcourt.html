Dorian Valcourt

Summary

Dorian Valcourt is an Ilmarian nobleman, scholar, and consulting investigator whose reputation has spread throughout the nation. Although frequently employed by the Interior Ministry, provincial constabularies, universities, and members of the aristocracy, he serves no institution directly and answers only to himself. Possessing a brilliant analytical mind and an obsessive devotion to evidence, Valcourt has solved murders, exposed conspiracies, dismantled criminal organisations, and uncovered political scandals that others considered unsolvable.

Unlike many scholars and occultists of his age, Valcourt neither dismisses nor embraces the supernatural. To him, all phenomena deserve investigation, regardless of how impossible they may appear. Whether faced with a serial killer, an ancient curse, a rogue Entitled, or rumours of forgotten gods, his response remains the same:

"Show me the evidence."

His growing career has drawn him into mysteries that challenge the foundations of reason itself, leading him towards truths buried beneath Ilmaria's history and beyond the limits of accepted magical theory.

Appearance

Dorian stands at approximately six feet in height, possessing a lean, almost gaunt frame more suited to libraries than battlefields. Years spent studying by candlelight and travelling across crime scenes have left him pale, with sharp features and perpetually tired eyes.

His dark brown hair is usually unkempt, often appearing as though he has run his hands through it repeatedly while contemplating a problem. He maintains a neatly trimmed moustache and short beard, more out of habit than vanity.

His eyes are a striking grey-blue, known for making people feel as though they are being examined rather than spoken to.

He typically dresses in dark, tailored Ilmarian noble fashion:

  • Long wool overcoats
  • High-collared waistcoats
  • Fine leather gloves
  • Walking cane
  • Pocket watch
  • Spectacles used only for reading

He carries a leather notebook almost everywhere he goes, often jotting down observations in shorthand intelligible only to himself.

Many describe him as looking more like a professor than a detective.

Personality

Dorian is highly intelligent, observant, and relentlessly curious.

He possesses little patience for superstition, incompetence, or emotional reasoning, preferring evidence above all else. While not intentionally rude, he often forgets social niceties when focused on an investigation.

Strengths

  • Exceptional deductive reasoning
  • Vast academic knowledge
  • Fearless pursuit of truth
  • Resistant to manipulation
  • Remarkably composed under pressure

Flaws

  • Obsessive
  • Insomniac
  • Arrogant regarding his intellect
  • Neglectful of his own health
  • Struggles to empathise with others

Despite his detached nature, he is not cruel. He genuinely believes that understanding the truth is the greatest service one can provide to the living and the dead alike.

One of his greatest fears is not death.
It is uncertainty.
Dorian can tolerate danger.
He cannot tolerate not knowing.

People of Interest

Lord Auguste Valcourt

Dorian's father and patriarch of House Valcourt.

A respected nobleman who expected his son to enter politics or government service. While proud of Dorian's accomplishments, he privately considers his son's profession beneath the dignity of their family.

Lady Celeste Valcourt

Dorian's younger sister.

One of the few people capable of speaking candidly to him without receiving a cutting remark in return. She often worries about his isolation and unhealthy obsessions.

Inspector Matthias Kruger

Senior Constabulary investigator.

Their relationship is complicated.

Kruger simultaneously respects, admires, and despises Dorian. He frequently requests Valcourt's assistance while resenting how often the nobleman solves cases that have frustrated official investigators for months.

Professor Elias Veyne

Scholar of history and arcane studies.

One of Dorian's oldest acquaintances and a frequent source of information regarding ancient cultures, magical phenomena, and forgotten civilisations.

The Red Archivist

An unidentified individual repeatedly connected to several unrelated investigations.

Little is known about them beyond their apparent interest in collecting forbidden texts and occult knowledge. Dorian has encountered traces of their involvement in multiple cases over the years.

Locations of Interest

Valcourt Manor

The ancestral estate of House Valcourt.

Located outside the Ilmarian capital, the manor serves as Dorian's legal residence, though he rarely spends time there.

The Valcourt Townhouse

Dorian's actual home.

An ageing noble townhouse within the capital city, filled with books, maps, case files, evidence lockers, and curiosities from countless investigations.

Most visitors describe it as organised chaos.

Ilmarian National Archives

One of Dorian's most frequented locations.

Many of his investigations begin amongst forgotten records, shipping manifests, census rolls, and centuries-old documents.

Saint Aurelia University

A prestigious academic institution.

Its libraries and scholars provide valuable resources during particularly complex investigations.

The Old Quarter

The oldest district of the capital.

A maze of narrow streets, hidden courtyards, and ancient foundations dating back centuries. Numerous criminal organisations, secret societies, and strange rumours originate here.

History

Dorian Valcourt was born into House Valcourt, an old noble family whose influence stretched back centuries within Ilmaria. From a young age he displayed an unusual intellect, often spending more time studying maps, books, and records than participating in noble social functions.

His education was extensive and included:

  • History
  • Law
  • Natural philosophy
  • Magical theory
  • Linguistics
  • Forensic medicine

By his early twenties he had gained a reputation amongst academics for solving historical and legal disputes through meticulous research and logical analysis.

His first major investigation occurred when a series of murders baffled both local authorities and magical experts. Through careful examination of evidence overlooked by others, Dorian identified the culprit and exposed corruption within the investigation itself.

Word of his success spread rapidly.

Over the following decade he became increasingly sought after by government officials, nobles, and law enforcement agencies throughout Ilmaria.

Numerous high-profile cases followed:

  • The Blackwater Murders
  • The Ashford Poisonings
  • The Night Market Conspiracy
  • The Cathedral Theft
  • The Severed Hand Society

With each solved mystery, his reputation grew.

So too did the nature of the cases.

Simple murders became occult murders.

Occult murders became ancient mysteries.

Ancient mysteries became encounters with things that should not exist.

In recent years Dorian has found himself investigating a disturbing pattern. Across Ilmaria and beyond, unrelated incidents appear connected by recurring symbols, forgotten languages, impossible architecture, and references to entities absent from every known historical record.

For the first time in his life, Dorian Valcourt has begun to encounter mysteries for which reason alone may not be enough.

And that possibility frightens him more than any monster ever could.
